---
title: "Endless"
author: "Roméon"
---

In all my innocence   
I learnt the alphabet to write poetry   
I learnt to walk to see the world   
But no one gave me directions   
Steps were only projections   
I broke limbs when I gave you mine   
How else would you be made for me?   
They say blue is sad   
So my sky cannot be blue   
I see the colour of our love   
When I look up   

I was scared of old age   
The thought of my image    
Wrinkled and grey    
A life of more respect and less love   
But now that you're here   
The end doesn't scare me anymore   
Because I know I'll be loved   
Matrimonial or death bed   
Now I am not as innocent    
I read the dictionary to curse    
I made so many steps and I'm scared    
That I am walking away from you    
Adventurers name their discoveries    
I don't have a good name so I'll call you mine   
I used to look in the mirror   
Now I look in your eyes   
Small as I am,    
I slide up and down your smile   
So please don't stop    
It's no fun when it's just a line    

Here I am, a poet  
and yet   
I think I learnt the alphabet just to say your name  
I can walk now,  
and yet  
You've become my world  
And I know I said I couldn't fly  
But I'm floating high  
Your fire burning my air  
If this is a lie I still want you  
The truth hurts anyway  
And if this is a dream, then I am Dream  
It may mean nothing when I wake   
or the colour of a weekend   
All I know is with you   
I am Endless   

~ Roméon 👉👈 ~
