---
title: "Guessing"
author: "Roméon"
---
Hey, I just wanted to say hi   
Say, do you know what you want   
With this one life?  

I know you're just eight    
But do you think life is about fate?    

When I look in your eyes     
I see you drowning in whys    
Seems we all share this same pie    
And everyone is so broken    
God, why is everyone so broken?    

We're all just looking for a little love    
But we say we have time    
to have fun, feel like a dove     
I can't even tell if we take it too far    
Neither do I know where to start     
It's all circular, and I'm dizzy     
I could ask for help, but I'm busy     

Does globalization mean no boundaries?    
We make we small to fit in     
Thinking happiness is tied to injuries     
Following the luminaries    

Hey, I know you're just twenty-eight    
But can people just walk in and out of our heart?     
 Like it's a milestone, not a cornerstone     
We are all just walking in the dark     
Hoping we stumble on someplace like home    
Or just anywhere we can leave a mark     
It seems we've made the pain and the hurt art    

Most of the time I'm in my mind     
Self-aware but no personality     
Borrowing it from what I consume     
Maybe that would make me a little cool     
But when I'm alone I feel cold     

We have dreams that give us nightmares    
Even our parents don't know what is right    
They are just older kids in the night     
Stuck between fight or flight     
And it feels like we can't be saved    
Can't even learn a lesson in school     
We are always repeating the same mistakes     

And now that we are fifty-eight     
I ask you once again     
What do you want death to find?    
when you don't expect much from life    
and your life expectancy conceives     
a dying soul that learns     
that time was only there to deceive     

We are all guessing, no concierge     
In this one life you are the pioneer     
Follow the leader till the edge     
and at ninety-eight     
this will have been a pledge    

 ~ Roméon🤓 ~
