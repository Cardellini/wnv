---
title: "Avatar"
author: "Roméon"
---
Frozen, cast in a block of ice  
Chosen, I never had a choice  
Heart was cold, mind was still  
Then she walked into my life   
And my sun began to rise   
Ice to water, melting    
Hot to hotter, sweating   
I’m dancing to your wave   
Shaped by everything you gave   
But then I start to sink   
Pulling me under your skin   
I’m drowning in you    
And when I reach   
Rock bottom beneath   
I know you are   

Solid, the ground that holds my feet   
Stolid, the fort to all my fleets  
You don’t have to see   
to know my next move   
You just have to feel   
And you will know the truth   
Every wall I built is falling   
And if your heart is made of stone  
I will cast my name in gold  
But when the foundation shakes  
and the sky breaks  
rocks ignite, and they start to rain  
We are a forest fire that’s   

Burning, everything in our wake   
Shining, between the flames    
We don’t know whether   
it’s easier to burn or break  
But we won’t change with the weather  
Our fire won’t grow cold  
Destructive and greedy  
The story of our unity  
Ablaze, but we won’t die  
Our love is our impunity  
Alive, and you are my why  
I’ll give you everything, even the sky  
We could be nomads who  

Soar, from cloud to cloud  
Blow, from town to town  
You are the breath I take  
The reason time is jealous  
If we glide or swerve  
Calm, I’ll keep my nerve  
If we can’t carry anything  
Can we just bring our memory  
Blow me away   
and I will come back  
Just so you could do it again  
I loved you before, now and after  
You are the elements  
And I am your avatar  

~ Roméon🌀 ~
