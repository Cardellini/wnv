---
title: "Vase"
author: "Roméon"
---
From dirt to craft       
Isn't that how we all start       
and the potter exerts        
Their life form in our being       
Beautiful patterns       
Unique to our soul       
Different colours        
we vases all glow        

But       
Someone somewhere said      
A vase is incomplete      
Without flowers to wilt      

We are hollow      
but maybe we are, by nature,      
incomplete by design,        
why else do we have this hole inside?     

A vessel for decoration      
Roses and daisies      
We're nothing without validation      
The promises of beauty      
We are invincible       
Empty without flowers       
We are only ourselves        
when we crack, break, and shatter       
It's never us they water      
It's their dishonesty      
They believe we need petals       
We are slaves in our dynasty     
So we believe it too       

What is a vase without flowers?     
Our beauty no matter how radiant      
Is nothing without obedience     
Conformity is our reality      
We've become a distraction      
With our destruction      
in the form of beautiful plants      

We are all vases      
Promised to be more      
But asked to be less       
Promised divinity       
For overwriting identity      

From craft to dirt       
The buyer is heartless       
When did we consent       
To sell our existence      
For wilting persistence?      

~ Roméon 🪴~
