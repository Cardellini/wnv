---
title: "Albedo"
author: "Roméon"
---

The sun rises over the Arctic    
A huge star of warmth     
Full of unspent love    
Burning itself for the whole     
It said hi with no reply     
Shine as it may,     
the cold wouldn't go away     
So it set at the end of the day      
Returned tomorrow with the same rays     

I never wished for global warming so much     
As I cross the sky hoping to melt     
The cold in her tundra to a belt      
My delusion is so high     
Because even from a million miles    
She still caught my eye     
 
I know we were never meant to be     
But I'm not asking for a lot     
If one degree is enough to graduate     
Then I just need one of yours to fluctuate    
Let me in, let me see, don’t let me be    
Because I am not a being     
Let me be because I am a beam    


I am not a creature; else I would    
adapt to your feature    
To your harsh conditions     
To your unlawful restitution    
I don’t need my love back    
I need it in your veins    
reflecting my heart’s crack    
If I could describe my pain    
It would be boundless like the dark   

Our communion would be an ice age   
Our love is already a movie   
and I am not Blue Sky    
Happy endings are where you lose me  
I could reign as sun, as aristocrat   
Yet if you were an acorn, I'd become Scrat   

Is this about Icarus?   
Yes, my love is dangerous   
But what is love    
If not preposterous    
If you had wings, would you-  
Because if I had limbs I would-  

So as I illuminate your white desert    
My heart breaks a little    
But my light reflects for albedo    
Why do I burn but you stay frozen    
My light golden and my heart swollen    

Maybe I only see myself in you   
Snow was always the color of the sun   
And mirrors don’t stay for love   
They stay because they can’t run   


 ~ Roméon☀️ ~
